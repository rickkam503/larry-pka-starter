# Team - AI team of agents

Human-readable profiles of the AI team — one file per member. The HR specialist (Nolan in the default team) owns these files. They complement the callable definitions in `.claude/agents/`: the `.claude/agents/` files are the operational identity; the profiles here are the introductory surface (you can hand one to someone outside the workspace as a "this is my team" intro).

This folder is empty in the starter kit. Profiles populate as you keep / retire specialists during onboarding. See `docs/getting-started.md` step 3.
